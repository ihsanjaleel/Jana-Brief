    How to read these figures

    Spending is the money paid for public works bills tagged to each ward on the BBMP works portal, in the old 198-ward system. All years are shown in 2022-23 prices, so rising construction costs do not make later years look bigger. Works that serve the whole city, such as flyovers, white-topping and major storm-water drains, and bills that cannot be placed in one ward are not counted. That was about 29% of spending in these years, so these figures are not all the money spent in your ward.

    There has been no census since 2011. Each year’s population is an estimate between Census 2011 and a 2025 projection, so the per-person figures are approximate. The change a year is the trend across all six years, not a comparison of two single years. These figures do not prove that any ward was treated unfairly. They show why a published, needs-based way of sharing money between wards matters.
